How to play

  1. Select a blue unit on the battlefield.
  2. Choose Move or Attack.
  3. Click a glowing legal target to queue an order.
  4. Submit the round. The red bot responds automatically.

One free move and one free attack per round. Scout moves exactly 3 hexes, infantry 2, heavy 1. Fog hides enemy positions outside your units’ sight.
