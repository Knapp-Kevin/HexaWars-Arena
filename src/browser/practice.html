How it works

Choose a Blue squad, then a highlighted destination. Drag or pinch to explore. Each round, move two distinct squads, then attack with one squad. Tap a unit to see allowed moves and attacks. Review your two slots, then resolve the round. Both sides act in initiative order. A higher AP bid determines priority within each movement/attack wave but consumes that AP. Unused free actions never generate AP in Maneuver. Further AP extras are not yet available in practice.

Scouts move exactly three hexes, infantry two, heavies one. A legal move must follow passable connected terrain. Attacks have each squad's own range and retaliation risks. This preview limits same-squad move-and-attack combinations that would leave the attack out of range.

The rules are the same in every match. Terrain blocks movement and line of sight; initiative bids settle movement and attack priority. Practice is currently local human-vs-AI and unranked.
